## `$ whoami`

Hey, I'm Lucas — a developer with a designer's eye and a data engineer's
habit of asking questions.

I like moving between different layers of a project: shaping an interface
with React, building APIs with Python, working with data, or writing a
small tool to make an annoying task disappear.

Most of my work revolves around **software development, data, and
automation**, but I'm not particularly interested in staying inside one
box. I enjoy learning by building things, experimenting with new ideas,
and understanding how the pieces fit together.

Outside the usual stack, I'm also into Linux, custom desktop setups,
self-hosted tools, and making my own workflow a little better. That's
part of what drives my personal projects: solving real problems,
exploring ideas, and occasionally building something just because
I want to see if I can.

Currently exploring the intersection of **AI, local-first software,
automation, and practical everyday tools**.
